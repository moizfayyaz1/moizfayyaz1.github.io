Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Khitta Reels is a private tool used only by the owner of the Khitta channel (@KhittaPK) to upload the channel's own videos to its own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API and TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to upload videos and their titles, descriptions and hashtags to the owner's accounts, to read the TikTok account's name and posting settings, and to read back the links of those posts.

What it collects

It does not collect, store or process personal data about anyone else. It does not read viewers' data, comments or messages.

How access is stored

The access tokens the owner grants are kept privately on the owner's own server and are never shared, sold or given to anyone.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google account (Security → Third-party access) or in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions).

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Khitta channel's pages (@KhittaPK).
